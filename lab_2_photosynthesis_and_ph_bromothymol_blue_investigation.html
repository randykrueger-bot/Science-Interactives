Photosynthesis and pH (Bromothymol Blue Investigation)

CO2 + H2O ⇌ H2CO3 (Carbonic Acid)
How BTB Works

When CO2 dissolves in water, it forms weak carbonic acid, lowering the pH and turning Bromothymol Blue (BTB) Yellow. When Elodea performs photosynthesis, it removes CO2, raising the pH and turning BTB Blue!
