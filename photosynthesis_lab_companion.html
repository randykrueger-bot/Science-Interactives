5. In 2–3 sentences, explain how a plant makes its own food (glucose). Be sure to mention the inputs (what goes in), the outputs (what comes out), and where inside the plant cell this happens.

Science Vocabulary Tracker (lights up as you write!):
📍 Chloroplast 💧 Water 🌫️ Carbon Dioxide ☀️ Light Energy 🍬 Glucose / Sugar 🫧 Oxygen
Example 7th-Grade Response:

"Photosynthesis happens inside the chloroplasts of a plant cell. During this process, the chloroplast takes in water, carbon dioxide, and light energy from the sun. It uses the light energy to rearrange those atoms into glucose (sugar) for food and releases oxygen as an output."
